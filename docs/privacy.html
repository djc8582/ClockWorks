Privacy Policy

Clockworks · Last updated June 15, 2026

The short version

Clockworks does not collect any data.

Clockworks is designed to work entirely on your device. We do not collect, store, transmit, or share any personal information. There are no accounts, no analytics, no advertising, and no tracking of any kind.

Information we collect

None. Clockworks does not gather personal information, usage data, or device identifiers. The app does not require an account and does not connect to any server we operate.

Your projects and content

Any music projects you create are stored locally on your device. They are never uploaded to us or any third party. When you choose to export a MIDI, audio, or PDF file, that file is handed to the standard iOS share sheet under your control — you decide where it goes.

Third-party services

Clockworks does not integrate any third-party analytics, advertising, or tracking services.

Children's privacy

Because Clockworks collects no personal information from anyone, it collects none from children. The app is suitable for all ages.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new "Last updated" date.
