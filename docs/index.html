Clockworks

Polyrhythmic music maker

Frequently asked questions

What is Clockworks?

Clockworks is a polyrhythmic composition tool. You layer rotating shapes on a clockface — each shape's sides become evenly-spaced beats — so combining different shapes creates intricate cross-rhythms with no math required.

How do I add a shape?

Tap anywhere on the canvas to add a shape, then use the bottom panel to change its number of sides, instrument, and scale. Press play to hear all shapes loop together.

Can I export my music?

Yes. Clockworks exports to MIDI, audio (WAV), and sheet music PDF using the share sheet, so you can continue your work in any DAW or notation app.

Does Clockworks require an account or internet connection?

No. Clockworks works fully offline and requires no account or sign-up. Everything runs on your device.

Where are my projects stored?

Projects are saved locally on your device. Nothing is uploaded to any server.
